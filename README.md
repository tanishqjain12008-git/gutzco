# Gutzco

Act as an expert and advanced website developer, create a one page website for a beverage brand that aims to solve problem of Gut through its drink.

Webiste sitemap Gutz co.

1.      Navbar

2.      Hero Section

3.      Our Story

4.      Product Showcase

5.       Footer

Design of the website:-
website should be very creative,premium looking, aesthetic, full of animation, colourful suited to genZ consumer. colour palette should match with the logo and product  (uploaded) of the company. Content should also sounds GenZ. website should be user-friendly, responsive,engaging,clean layout suited to GenZ consumers.

Functionality of the website:-
so if a consumer clicks on "our story" option in menu bar , he will move to our story section through scroll animation.

Detailed description of each section:-

1. Navbar + hero section
create a navbar and hero section such that navbar comes over hero section in a transparent way . for reference of this type of design- https://jmj1961.com/

in navbar there should be logo (uploaded)in centre and our story menu option in left and Product menu option in right. these 3 would centrally aligned.
In hero section, put a video (uploaded) without reducing its quality. 

2.  Our Story section
This section should be very creative that depicts the story behind starting this brand.creativity may in the form of diagrams , visuals or anything but very creative.
story is :- founder wants to solve  gut problem  that people face in their life through launching this drink.

3. Product Section:-
this section should showcase product in a very creative way with headings,paragraph,and button- Buy Now . when user click on "Buy Now" button he would move to checkout page.
